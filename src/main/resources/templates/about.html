About the Interview Selection System

The Interview Selection System is a web-based platform designed to support the admission and interview process for students applying for Advanced Level education opportunities.

The system provides a centralized platform where students can register, submit applications, receive interview information and check their selection results.

School administrators and interview panel members can manage applications, organize interviews, evaluate applicants and make selection decisions through a structured digital process.

Our Purpose

To make the student interview and selection process more efficient, transparent, organized and accessible through digital technology.

What Our System Provides

Student Registration

Students can create an account and begin their application process online.

Online Applications

Applicants can submit their educational information and preferred A/L stream.

Interview Management

Interview schedules and candidate information can be managed digitally.

Candidate Evaluation

Interview panel members can record marks and evaluate candidates.

Student Selection

Candidate results can be processed according to the defined selection criteria.

Notifications

Students can receive important information about interviews and selection results.
