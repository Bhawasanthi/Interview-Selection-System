A Smarter Way to Manage Student Selection

The Interview Selection System is a web-based platform developed to support schools in managing applications and interviews for students seeking Advanced Level education opportunities.

Students can register through the system, submit their application information, receive interview details and check their selection status online.

School administrators and interview panel members can efficiently manage applicants, interviews, evaluations and selection decisions through one centralized system.

Fair & Transparent

Our system supports an organized and transparent process for handling student applications and interviews.

Our System Features

Everything needed to manage the student selection process.

Student Registration

Students can create an account and begin their application through the online system.

Online Application

Applicants can provide their educational information and preferred A/L stream.

Interview Scheduling

Interview information can be organized and communicated to selected applicants.

Candidate Evaluation

Interview panel members can record candidate marks and evaluation information.

Student Selection

Candidate results can be evaluated according to the defined selection criteria.

Notifications

Students can receive important information about interviews and selection results.
