How to get a Gmail App Password

  1. Sign in to the school Gmail account (e.g. issforschool@gmail.com).
  2. Turn on 2-Step Verification at myaccount.google.com/security.
  3. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords, enter a name such as Interview Selection System and click Create.
  4. Copy the 16-character password Google shows and paste it into App Password here.
  5. Click Save & Test Connection, then send a test email.
