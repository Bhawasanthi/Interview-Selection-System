President's College Embilipitiya

R/Emb/President's College is located in New Town, Embilipitiya, in the Sabaragamuwa Province of Sri Lanka.

The school is included in the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Education Department's school information for the Embilipitiya area.

The school provides education from Grade 1 to Grade 13 and is identified as a 1AB school in the published education information.

About the School

Publicly available information about President's College Embilipitiya states that the school was established in 1977 as New Town Vidyalaya during the development of the Embilipitiya area.

The school was associated with the development of the area under the River Valley Development Board. The institution later became known as R/Emb/Madhya Maha Vidyalaya and subsequently developed into President's College.

Today, President's College is an important educational institution serving students in the Embilipitiya area.
